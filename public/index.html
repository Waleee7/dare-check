What we don't do

No hacking, no logins, no poking at hidden files. One visit to your homepage and a look at your public DNS records: the same things any browser can see.

002 / How the grade works

14 checks · 100 points

Each check is worth points. A pass earns all of them, a warning half, a fail none. Checks that can't run don't count. 90 or more is an A, 80 a B, 65 a C, 50 a D. A site without working HTTPS gets an F no matter what, because everything else depends on it.
